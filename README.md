# 🧩 VCV Rack 2 Pro  
**Modular Synthesizer Environment by VCV**

**VCV Rack 2 Pro** is a modular synthesis platform that brings the power of Eurorack hardware into the digital world.  
With hundreds of modules and full DAW integration, it’s a playground for sound designers, experimental musicians, and synthesis enthusiasts.

## ✨ Features
- **Modular System** – patch virtual cables between hundreds of modules  
- **VST/AU Plugin Support** – integrate directly with your DAW  
- **Expansive Module Library** – 2000+ free and premium modules  
- **Open-Source Ecosystem** – create or install custom modules  
- **High-Performance Engine** – optimized audio and GPU rendering  
- **Formats:** VST2 / VST3 / AU / Standalone (Windows, macOS, Linux)

## 🧠 Why VCV Rack 2 Pro
VCV Rack 2 Pro offers a fully modular sound design environment that mirrors real-world Eurorack systems.  
It’s ideal for learning modular synthesis, exploring complex patching, or crafting experimental sonic landscapes.

## 💻 Requirements
- Windows 10+ / macOS 10.13+ / Linux  
- 64-bit DAW supporting VST2, VST3, or AU  
- 8 GB RAM recommended  
- Graphics card with OpenGL 2.1 or higher
